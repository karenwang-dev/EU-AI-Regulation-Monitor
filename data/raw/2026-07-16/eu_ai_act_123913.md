# Factsheet - Action Plan on Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence

- FACTSHEET / INFOGRAPHIC
- Publication 07 July 2026

The European Commission has presented an Action Plan for a structured response to address the risks and harness the opportunities of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) models for cybersecurity.

![A shield symbol with text "AI" in it, surrounded by a digital grid, suggesting artificial intelligence and cybersecurity.](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/styles/newsroom_large/public/newsroom/items/GettyImages2222990006Just_Super__E_ratio32_gD7qS1Iak7XUO1sWCc7EHScjihA_240475.jpg?itok=nrhqsXK_) GettyImages ©Just Super - E+


Building on the EU’s unique legal framework for AI, which is at once risk-based and bound by comprehensive cybersecurity rules, the Action Plan will bring together Member States, industry and EU-level organisations to strengthen the cybersecurity of our digital landscape against the vulnerabilities posed by advanced AI.

Read more about the [Action Plan](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/eu-action-plan-cybersecurity-and-artificial-intelligence).
