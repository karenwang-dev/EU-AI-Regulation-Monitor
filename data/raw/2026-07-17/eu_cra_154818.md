# Cyber Resilience Act - Member States

Member States play an essential role in the implementation of the CRA. In particular, they are responsible for the management of conformity assessment bodies.


Further, they monitor the market of products with digital elements, perform inspections and take corrective and restrictive measures when needed.

## What are the main rules?

The CRA implements an ex-post enforcement model for placing products on the EU market from the angle of cybersecurity. The manufacturer ensures that the product is compliant with the essential requirements established in the CRA and places the product on the market under their sole responsibility. Member States are responsible for enforcement. In other words, they strategically verify the products placed on the EU market and may request corrective or even restrictive measures to all the economic operators in the supply chain (manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers and distributors). Member States also assess and monitor conformity assessment bodies under the CRA. For that purpose, they designate Notifying authorities. In order to ensure a uniform approach and consistent implementation of their activities, Member States coordinated in different foras.

Beyond market surveillance and conformity assessment, Member States contribute to the development of guidance and more generally to raising awareness about the CRA at national level, as well as, at European level, as part of the CRA Expert Group. Furthermore, they contribute to the development of implementing regulations for the CRA as members of the [Product Security Committee](https://ec.europa.eu/transparency/comitology-register/screen/committees/C130100).

## Relevant cooperation bodies

- List of designated [Notifying Authorities](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/single-market-compliance-space/notified-bodies/notifying-authorities?filter=legislationId:167953) (NAs) and [Market Surveillance Authorities](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/single-market-compliance-space/market-surveillance/ms-authorities?filter=legislationId:9021)
- The [Administrative Cooperation Groups (AdCos)](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/goods/building-blocks/market-surveillance/organisation/adcos_en) are the sectorial administrative cooperation groups of market surveillance. They constitute a cooperation forum among national authorities.
- The [Working Group of Notifying Authorities](https://circabc.europa.eu/ui/group/4db8f867-8ac9-49dd-8a01-bbf3bdf75b36) (restricted) is responsible to assess, designate, notify and monitor conformity assessment bodies under the CRA.
- The [CRA ADCO (Market surveillance authorities representatives)](https://circabc.europa.eu/ui/group/abde7d47-c03b-41e4-849a-c7baf0ae5f0f) (restricted)  is the cooperation group of market surveillance authorities under the CRA.
