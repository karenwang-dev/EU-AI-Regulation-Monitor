# New Digital Product Passport (DPP) web page launched

Supporting Europe's transition to more transparent product information

- News announcement
- 17 July 2026
- Directorate-General for Internal Market, Industry, Entrepreneurship and SMEs
- 2 min read

![](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/styles/oe_theme_medium_no_crop/public/2026-07/DPP%20news%20section%20banner_v4%201.png?itok=YAai3RPI)

The European Commission has launched the new [Digital Product Passport web page](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport_en), providing economic operators, public authorities, consumers and other stakeholders with a central source of information on one of the key initiatives supporting Europe's transition to more transparent product information.

Developed as part of the implementation of the [Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj/eng) (ESPR), the DPP will make important product information more accessible throughout a product's lifecycle. The new web page explains how the DPP will work, what it means for different stakeholder groups, and how implementation will progress across product sectors.

### A reference point for DPP information

The page has been designed as the entry point for everyone interested in the DPP. Visitors to the site can find

- an introduction to the DPP and its objectives
- explanations of how the DPP works throughout a product's lifecycle
- dedicated information tailored to economic operators, consumers, public authorities and other value chain actors that will expand during implementation
- the latest news and implementation updates
- news on past and upcoming webinars and events
- frequently asked questions
- guidance and supporting documentation

As implementation progresses, the landing page will continue to expand with new guidance, legislative updates, technical resources, webinars, and sector-specific information.

### Supporting product information transparency and informed consumer choices

The DPP is designed to improve transparency across value chains by providing product information in digital form. Depending on the product group, a DPP may include information such as

- product composition
- circularity information
- environmental impact
- usage and maintenance
- safety information
- end-of-life guidance
- regulatory compliance

Making this information more accessible will help improve information sharing, support more informed purchasing decisions, facilitate repair and recycling, and contribute to compliance with EU legislation.

### Progressive implementation

The DPP will be introduced progressively through product-specific legislation under the ESPR framework or via sector-specific legislation. Implementation will begin with certain types of batteries (including industrial batteries, batteries for light means of transport and electric vehicles batteries), with additional product groups expected to follow over time, including textile apparel, iron and steel and construction products. The information available within each DPP will depend on the relevant legislative requirements for each product group.

### Explore the new web page

The DPP web page will evolve alongside the implementation of the DPP framework, providing stakeholders with up-to-date information, guidance and resources as new product groups are introduced.

Whether you are a business preparing for future requirements, a public authority supporting implementation, a consumer interested in more transparent product information or another actor across the value chain, the landing page offers a single place to stay informed.
