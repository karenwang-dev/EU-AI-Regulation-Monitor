# Raw materials, metals, minerals and forest-based industries

**Securing a sustainable supply of raw materials is a key priority for the EU. Raw materials, such as metals and minerals or forest-based materials, have become increasingly important to the EU's economy, growth, and competitiveness. More than 30 million jobs in the EU and many key economic sectors such as automotive, aerospace, and renewable energy are dependent on a sustainable supply of raw materials. Raw materials are particularly crucial for the development of modern environmentally friendly technologies and a strong European industrial base. Without them, there wouldn’t be any smartphones, laptops, or cars.**

## Industries

The Commission's objective is to promote, within the EU’s wider industrial policy, the [competitiveness](https://ec.europa.eu/growth/industry/competitiveness_en) of industries related to raw materials

- [Mining industries](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/minerals-and-non-energy-extractive-industries_en) comprising of minerals and non-energy extractive industries
- [Metal industries](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/metal-industries_en) comprising of [non-ferrous metals](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/metal-industries/non-ferrous-metals_en) such as aluminium, copper, and zinc - and ferrous materials such as [steel](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/metal-industries/steel_en)
- [Non-metallic mineral products industries](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/non-metallic-products-and-industries_en) comprising of the production of [cement and lime](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/non-metallic-products-and-industries/cement-and-lime_en), [ceramics](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/non-metallic-products-and-industries/ceramics_en), [glass](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/non-metallic-products-and-industries/glass_en)
- [Forest based industries](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/forest-based-industries_en) consisting of four major sectors: [wood working](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/forest-based-industries/woodworking_en), [furniture](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/forest-based-industries/furniture-industry_en), [paper and pulp manufacturing](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/forest-based-industries/pulp-and-paper-industry_en), and [printing](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/related-industries/forest-based-industries/printing-industry_en)
