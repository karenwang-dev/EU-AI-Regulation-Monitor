# AI Act Scientific Panel

The Scientific Panel advises the AI Office and national authorities on the implementation the AI Act and assessment of the impacts and risks of General-Purpose AI (GPAI) models.


The Scientific Panel is an expert advisory body established by the European Commission in accordance with Article 68 of the AI Act in conjunction with Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/454. 60 world-class independent AI experts have been recruited to join the AI Act Scientific Panel. These leading technical experts will directly support the implementation and enforcement of the [AI Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai).

The core responsibilities of the Scientific Panel include alerting the AI Office to systemic risks, advising on GPAI classification and evaluation methodologies and supporting market surveillance activities. This panel is complimentary to the [Advisory Forum](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-advisory-forum), which provides broader stakeholder input to the Commission and the [AI Board](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-board) from across industry, civil society, and academia.

The selected experts contribute with their expertise across a broad range of areas, including GPAI capability evaluation, risk assessment methodologies, technical risk mitigations, misuse/deployment risks, cyber offence risks, provider cybersecurity, emergent systemic risks, and compute measurement.

Besides ensuring top-tier expertise, the selection also considered geographic balance (at most 3 nationals from each country) and gender balance, giving preference to the underrepresented gender when candidates were equally qualified.

The experts are appointed to the panel for a 24-month term, renewable, in personal capacity. Most experts work in academia, while others work in organisations linked to the public sector, independent research, or civil society. The experts have each signed a [declaration of confidentiality (PDF)](https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/129022) and a [declaration of commitment (PDF)](https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/129023) in advance of taking on work and activities as part of the Scientific Panel. A chair and a vice-chair will be nominated by the members and selected by the Commission upon nomination.
