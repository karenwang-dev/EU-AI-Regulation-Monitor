## Why join DVB?

1


### Shape the standards, don't just implement them

DVB specifications are freely available for anyone to use – but Members are the ones who define them. Joining means you can contribute to defining the commercial requirements that underpin DVB specs.

2


### Stay ahead of the transition to IP

DVB’s current work – on hybrid service discovery, IP delivery, addressable advertising, accessibility and more – is where the industry is heading. Being inside that process means being ready for the transition.

3


### Work with the whole value chain in one room

DVB is unique in bringing together content providers, platform operators, CE manufacturers, technology vendors and regulators.  If your organization has a stake in media distribution, we have a place for you.
