[Rule of law report strengthens EU democracy and fundamental rights](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/rule-law-report-strengthens-eu-democracy-and-fundamental-rights-2026-07-17_en)

The 2026 Rule of Law report shows how well the rule of law is holding up across the EU and 4 potential EU members: Albania, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia.
